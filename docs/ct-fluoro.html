Parts of the panoramic (OPG) unit

3D room
  • Rotating armSemicircular gantry that carries the tube and sensor once around the head.
  • X-ray tubeSource at the leading end of the arm; it emits a narrow fan beam.
  • Slit collimatorVertical jaws that narrow the beam to the thin slice the sensor records.
  • Detector sensorSlit sensor opposite the tube; each column becomes one strip of the panorama.
  • Head positionerBite block and chin rest that align the dental arch in the focal trough.
  • Column & liftMotorised mast that raises the whole arm to the patient's height.
